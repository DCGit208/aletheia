## 1. Why this matrix exists

The nine first-pass chapters were drafted and then checked for coverage of the leaflet. That was not enough to validate the book's logic, because the foundation of Hebrews 11:1 / *hypostasis* had been assigned to Book II. This matrix tests a different question: does each major proposition have a clear place in the corrected argument, and can the reader see why the next proposition follows?

The revised architecture must meet both standards:

- **Coverage:** all material teachings and questions in the Aletheia leaflet, and all relevant owner notes, remain engaged.
- **Dependency:** the reader is led through a reasoned argument, not a list of terms and proof-texts. The foundational proposition must govern the later chapters, and the later chapters must substantively develop—not merely repeat—the foundation.

## 2. The argument has two related sequences

A crucial distinction is needed between the **order of explanation** and the **conceptual relationships being explained**.

### 2.1 Order of explanation

This is the reader-facing route through Book I:

**Hebrews 11:1 / hypostasis → lexical and contextual investigation → Aletheia pistis / state of children → design and Sonship → divine nature and pleasing God → deviation and Christ's person/work → redemption and righteousness → heirship and oneness → image/glory and present reality → Book II's evidence inquiry.**

This order places the foundation first, because that is the owner's explicit direction.

### 2.2 Conceptual relationships

The owner's notes also preserve a broader chain:

**God → God's nature → image and likeness → humanity's original design/state → Sonship/heirship → faith → pleasing God/good report → deviation/coming short of glory → Christ as representation → redemption → justification/righteousness → restoration/back on course → Sonship/joint-heirship → divine nature → oneness → present reality → substance → evidence.**

This is not discarded. It is a conceptual map to investigate. Its placement of “substance” near the end describes a cumulative relationship in the developing thesis; it does not override the owner's later direction that the *hypostasis* proposition must serve as the foundational opening of Book I. The book can establish a governing foundation at the beginning and then demonstrate, deepen, and return to its implications throughout the argument.

## 5. Dependency questions to answer during the next pass

The following questions govern the ongoing substantive revision and must be answered or explicitly left open before publication approval:

1. **Definition and context:** What does Hebrews 11:1 actually assert grammatically, and what are the defensible contextual senses of *hypostasis* here?
2. **Author-internal comparison:** What does Hebrews 1:3's use of *hypostasis* contribute, and what does Hebrews 3:14 caution against?
3. **Source connection:** How does the Hebrews 11:1 foundation illuminate the leaflet's explicit *pistis* / credence / state teaching without pretending the leaflet contains a Hebrews 11:1 word study?
4. **State/nature argument:** What reasoning connects substance/reality with the person's underlying nature/state in the owner's thesis? Which premises come from lexical evidence, which from the leaflet, which from other Scripture, and which from owner synthesis?
5. **Design:** How do Genesis 1:26–28 and the image/likeness cluster contribute to the nature/state argument?
6. **Sonship:** How do adoption, heirship, good report, pleasing God, and the Father's declaration relate to faith as state?
7. **Deviation/restoration:** How do coming short of glory, redemption, justification, and righteousness relate to the original design without reducing one term to another?
8. **Christ:** How does the Son's *hypostasis* relation in Hebrews 1:3 connect with the owner's representation thesis and the Aletheia image/glory cluster? Where does the reasoning require an owner decision?
9. **Oneness/present reality:** How do John 17:20–23 and 1 John 4:17 contribute without being reduced to slogans or detached from context?
10. **Series boundary:** What must Book I establish so that Book II can examine evidence without repeating or repairing the foundation?

## 6. Quality gates for continuing revision

The following gates govern the active substantive revision and must be rechecked before publication approval:

- every source-map item has a primary chapter and necessary cross-links;
- every owner note has a substantive home, not just a mention;
- every chapter has a defined question, premises, reasoning steps, conclusion, and transition;
- the lexical argument is contextual, not etymology-only;
- no old draft statement contradicts the owner's corrected Book I / Book II boundary;
- the source, Scripture, synthesis, and owner-formulation categories remain explicit;
- the unresolved Hebrews 1:8 conclusion and lexical formulations remain visible for owner review;
- the companion app content and all first-pass drafts remain preserved.

**Current conclusion:** The foundation-first direction is implemented in ten coordinated working manuscripts. The present phase is substantive argument and source-fidelity revision, not merely generic prose polishing. The quality gates remain active and must be checked against the revised prose as well as the original source maps.

### Structural consequence

The ten-chapter structure is the current working recommendation, not a locked decision. The continuous-book revision has confirmed that the sequence can be followed from the foundation through *pistis* / Sonship, design, Psalm 82, divine nature, Christ's coming, redemption/righteousness, heirship, oneness, and the image in Christ. It still requires a full line-by-line continuity pass and owner review of unresolved doctrinal questions. After inspecting the first-pass drafts, the Hebrews 11:2, 5–6 / Matthew 3:17 Sonship-and-pleasing-God inquiry is best integrated into the *pistis* chapter because it directly extends the source's faith/state/Sonship argument; a standalone chapter would risk repeating that same argument after the design chapters. This remains open to revision if the full prose rewrite reveals a genuine independent movement that cannot be handled coherently in Chapter 2.
