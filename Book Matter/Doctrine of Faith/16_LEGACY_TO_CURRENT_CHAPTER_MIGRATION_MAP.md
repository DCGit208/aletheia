The 2015 Aletheia leaflet remains the controlling doctrinal source. This map does not treat the older app companion as a second doctrinal authority; it records how its teaching material is carried into the expanded treatment.

## Control principles

1. The 2015 leaflet, *Aletheia Libraries 1*, is the controlling Aletheia doctrinal source for this work. The current book must engage its teaching fully and faithfully.
2. The old eight-chapter companion is preserved verbatim in `15_LEGACY_EIGHT_CHAPTER_COMPANION_ARCHIVE.md`. It is a migration reference, not a substitute for the controlling leaflet.
3. The ten-chapter manuscript is an expansion and reorganization, not a claim that every sentence of the former companion must be reproduced verbatim.
4. Where a former paragraph is not carried over word-for-word, the editor must confirm that its substantive teaching is addressed in the new chapter or deliberately identified as material outside Book I's scope.
5. Distinguish direct Scripture quotation, the leaflet's wording, lexical explanation, and the author's doctrinal exposition. This is attribution and accuracy control; it does not qualify the leaflet's authority.
6. Preserve the established doctrinal direction: faith is our state by design; Jesus represents who we are by Design; representation and accomplishment belong together; sons are natural heirs; the Father's will and pleasure are for sons to operate in their designed state; justification and redemption restore original design.
