## Aletheia source quotation controls

The following are not Bible translations and must be checked against the 2015 Aletheia leaflet:
- *pistis* / “credence” and faith defining the state of children of God.
- “true nature” and acceptance/approval from creation.
- *’ĕlōhîm* described by the leaflet as “plural: Trinity.”
- “Like God did God make man” and the leaflet's Life Application Bible parenthetical attribution.
- Romans 3:23–24 gloss “deviation from true nature.”
- *dikaioō* printed gloss “render just or innocent (free from evil or guilt).”
- *dikaiosynē* explanation: equity, impartiality to and conformity with God's nature; “original: back on course.”
- *apolutrōsis*: “redemption; ransom in full.”
- The leaflet's question about Hebrews 1:8 is preserved in the source record but abandoned as a required owner inference.

## Final proof workflow

1. Check each quotation in the individual chapter file against the selected Bible edition.
2. Record the verified edition/version and exact reference.
3. Check every quotation again after layout, including punctuation, capitalization, italics, line breaks, and quotation marks.
4. Confirm that TLB credit appears on the copyright page and that the quoted TLB wording remains within the applicable permissions conditions.
5. Refresh continuous manuscript 11 after any chapter-level correction.
6. Do not mark the book publication-ready until every row has passed and the owner has reviewed the final designed proof.
