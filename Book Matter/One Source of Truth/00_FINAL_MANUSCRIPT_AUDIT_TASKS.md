## TASK 03 — Argument Progression Audit

**Status: PASS — with documentation-state corrections carried forward**

### Verified Findings

The governing documents define the manuscript as a **cumulative investigation**, not a collection of independent essays. The locked progression is:

> **Truth → Reality → Knowledge → Evidence → Reason and rational warrant → Ultimate authority → God and the ground of truth → Divine revelation → What the Bible is → Graphē/Holy Writ → How revelation was given → Witness and testimony → Writing and inspiration → Authentication → Christ → Apostolic testimony → Canon → Inspiration and infallibility → Christ's treatment of Scripture → “The scripture cannot be broken” → “Thy word is truth” → Graphē as primary written authority → Human interpretation and error → Tergiversation, accepted heresies, aridity, unskillfulness → Scholarly labour, reasonability, skill, rightly dividing → Readiness for the Six First Principles.**

The locked Movement objectives preserve the required dependency structure:

- **Movement I** establishes the epistemic problem: truth, knowledge, evidence, reason, judgment, and ultimate authority.
- **Movement II** addresses the ultimate ground of truth and the character of God relevant to trustworthy revelation.
- **Movement III** defines the Bible and introduces the Graphē/Holy Writ distinction before sweeping claims about biblical authority.
- **Movement IV** examines how the written record was given: divine communication, revelation, inspiration, and written testimony.
- **Movement V** addresses authentication and moves from evidence toward rational warrant.
- **Movement VI** places Christ at the center of the historical and revelatory case and develops apostolic testimony.
- **Movement VII** develops Scripture, canon, inspiration, infallibility, and written authority as conclusions dependent upon the preceding groundwork.
- **Movement VIII** tests the established doctrine against Christ's own treatment of the Graphē and culminates in the claim that the Graphē is primary written authority.
- **Movement IX** then turns from establishing authority to the problem of mishandling authoritative truth, which is an appropriate change from **“What is authoritative?”** to **“How can authoritative truth be mishandled?”**
- **Movement X** develops the intellectual capacity required to engage that authoritative text responsibly and culminates in readiness for the Six First Principles.

The methodology explicitly requires:

> **Question → Definitions → Premises → Evidence → Reasoning → Conclusion → Next Question**

and states that no major conclusion should be introduced before its required premises have been established.

### Important Boundary Finding

A complete line-by-line argument audit of every sentence in Chapters 1–85 is a later-level content audit; this task is the **macro-argument progression audit**. The available repository architecture, locked TOC, methodology, and the completed Movement X sequence support the conclusion that the macro-argument is structurally cumulative and correctly directed.

The supporting architecture documents contain stale pre-Chapter-85 progress statements. These do not alter the argument itself, but they can mislead future writers about the current state of the argument. They remain scheduled for synchronization under Task 13.

### Task 03 Conclusion

**PASS — the manuscript's macro-argument progression is coherent and cumulative from the epistemic opening through the Graphē gateway to the Six First Principles.**

**No architectural reordering is presently indicated by this audit.**

**Deliverable:** Macro argument progression assessment established.

## TASK 05 — Core Terminology & Definition Audit

**Status: PASS — no material terminology or definition defect identified; continued local definition checks remain appropriate during Tasks 06–09**

### Verified Findings

The governing methodology establishes the manuscript's principal conceptual distinctions rather than permitting terminology to drift:

- **Truth** is treated as an ontological question: what is true/real.
- **Knowledge** is treated epistemologically: how truth can be known.
- **Evidence / warrant** is treated evidentially: what warrants concluding that something is true.
- **Interpretation** is treated hermeneutically: how an authoritative truthful text is correctly understood.
- **Scripture / Graphē / Holy Writ / written Word** are not casually collapsed into whatever a commentator says about them; the methodology expressly requires distinguishing what Scripture says from what someone says Scripture means and from what the author infers.

Chapter 82 explicitly distinguishes quotation, interpretation, inference, application, context, scope, and related categories. Chapter 83 carries the source/interpreter distinction into the concept of the workman. Chapter 84 makes definition auditing and distinction central to interpretation. Chapter 85 preserves source/subject, Graphē/interpretation, and gateway/First-Principles distinctions.

### Definition-Control Finding

The manuscript does not merely repeat preferred vocabulary. Its methodology requires:

> **Define it → Distinguish it → Establish premises → Present evidence → Reason → State explicit versus inferred → Draw warranted conclusion.**

That control is materially consistent with the terminology used in the verified later chapters.

This is not presently a defect. Tasks 06–09 should continue checking that a specialized meaning is not silently transferred between categories.

## TASK 06 — Scripture & Context Audit

**Status: PASS — strong contextual controls verified; no material Scripture-context defect identified in the audited Movement X material**

### Verified Findings

The manuscript's governing methodology explicitly requires the distinction between:

- what Scripture says;
- what a commentator says Scripture means;
- what the author infers from Scripture.

The late-manuscript chapters operationalize that rule rather than merely stating it.

Chapter 74 repeatedly warns against sloganizing individual verses, emphasizes surrounding and immediate context, and identifies speaker, audience, conditions, and paragraph-level meaning as relevant to responsible understanding.

Chapter 78 provides an especially clear lexical-context control:

> **Grammar identifies the form. Lexicon identifies the semantic range. Context determines the contextual sense.**

It also explicitly prevents the lexical meaning of σπουδάζω from being treated as though one English rendering automatically determines its meaning in every occurrence.

Chapters 79–81 keep Hebrews 5:11–14 as the governing paragraph for exercise, maturity, skill, babe, and unskillfulness rather than treating isolated phrases as independent doctrines.

Chapter 82 makes contextual handling an explicit requirement of “rightly dividing,” including:

- speaker;
- audience;
- subject;
- scope;
- conditions;
- qualifiers;
- immediate context;
- larger context;
- genre;
- quotation versus endorsement;
- interpretation versus inference.

Chapter 83 carries those same controls into the workman's responsibility, explicitly warning that removing conditions, negatives, or scope changes the proposition.

Chapter 84 formalizes the contextual method: immediate context first, followed by larger context where required, while warning that “more context” is not automatically “better context.”

Chapter 85 carries the same discipline into preparation for the Six First Principles, explicitly requiring examination of context, scope, surrounding argument, and distinctions within terms such as baptisms and faith.

The supplied **BIBLE INTRODUCTION** independently supports the contextual direction used in these chapters. Its material on “Rightly Dividing Truth” connects 2 Timothy 2:15 with *orthotomeō*, correct meaning/application, gathering meaning from Scripture itself, common sense and reason, grammatical rules, and attention to literal versus figurative language. fileciteturn300file1

This is significant because the manuscript is not importing a contextual methodology that contradicts the supplied source. It is developing the source's stated interpretive concerns into the more systematic discipline of Movement X.

### Qualification

This audit establishes **contextual discipline**, not automatic correctness of every individual exegetical conclusion. A later Scripture-level audit can still identify a specific passage where an application, inference, or contextual conclusion needs correction.

### Task 06 Conclusion

**PASS — the audited manuscript demonstrates strong Scripture-context discipline, preserves speaker/audience/scope/condition distinctions, and is consistent with the supplied Bible Introduction on the governing interpretive principles.**

## TASK 07 — Evidence & Source Integration Audit

**Status: PASS — source hierarchy and evidence discipline are strongly integrated; no material source-authority defect identified**

### Verified Findings

The master Source Integration and Evidence Protocol provides an unusually explicit control system for the manuscript's sources.

It distinguishes:

1. **Graphē / Holy Writ**
2. **Biblical textual witnesses**
3. **Historical and testimonial evidence**
4. **Lexical sources**
5. **Historical sources**
6. **Commentaries/reference works**
7. **Philosophical and theological arguments**

The protocol also requires every significant source claim to be classified as direct biblical assertion, biblical inference, historical claim, lexical claim, philosophical claim, theological formulation, interpretive claim, or evidential conclusion.

Most importantly, the evidence protocol requires the manuscript to ask:

> **Evidence of what?**

and then examine relevance, directness, independence, reliability, alternatives, degree of support, additional premises, and whether those premises have themselves been established.

### Manuscript Verification

Chapter 74 explicitly distinguishes source material from warranted conclusion and states that the Aletheia source's identification of the “scholarly impetus” must itself be examined rather than simply inherited.

Chapter 77 develops a particularly important evidence distinction:

> **Source → Quotation → Proposition → Meaning → Inference → Conclusion**

It recognizes that an accurate quotation can establish what a source says without automatically establishing every conclusion a quoter attaches to it.

Chapter 78 applies source discipline to lexical research. It distinguishes lexical range from contextual force and explicitly warns that lexical definitions do not themselves supply the whole theological conclusion.

Chapter 82 integrates the supplied Bible Introduction into the interpretive argument while retaining the distinction between its observations and the manuscript's warranted conclusions.

Chapter 84 formalizes source tracking, source hierarchy, commentary audit, lexical evidence, historical evidence, and the requirement that the strength of language correspond to the strength of warrant.

### Bible Introduction Integration

The supplied **BIBLE INTRODUCTION.pdf** is being used as a substantive source rather than as decorative citation. Its material on 2 Timothy 2:15, *orthotomeō*, precise meaning, correct application, grammatical rules, and literal/figurative language is explicitly integrated in Chapter 82. fileciteturn300file1

This integration is appropriately controlled: the manuscript does not simply say that the Bible Introduction establishes a doctrine. It extracts the relevant proposition and subjects it to the manuscript's own source/evidence protocol.

### Aletheia Libraries Integration

The **Aletheia Libraries 1.pdf** remains part of the source universe, but the manuscript's methodology does not treat Aletheia as an automatic doctrinal authority. The protocol explicitly requires proposition identification, definition, evidence, reasoning, biblical correspondence, inference testing, and determination of the source's actual contribution before incorporation.

That is the correct relationship for a secondary research source.

### Anti-Circularity Finding

The evidence protocol explicitly prohibits:

> **The Bible is true because the Bible says it is true.**

It requires separation of internal biblical claim, external corroboration, historical authentication, and cumulative argument. This is a major strength of the evidence architecture.

### Task 07 Conclusion

**PASS — the manuscript has a coherent and explicit evidence/source hierarchy, uses secondary materials as research resources rather than replacement authorities, and preserves the distinction between source claim, evidence, inference, and conclusion.**

No material correction is presently required.

**Deliverable:** Evidence and source-integration control established.
