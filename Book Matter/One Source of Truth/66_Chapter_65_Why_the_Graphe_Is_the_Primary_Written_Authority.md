# 65.3 Define the Question: Primary Written Authority

**Primary written authority**, in this chapter, is the dedicated account of the Graphē’s standing as the decisive written measure among written authorities for knowing, testing, reasoning through, teaching, discerning, and living truth, given that the Father’s word has already been identified as truth and that the writing has already been shown to be unbreakable because of what it is.

The phrase is easily abused. In ordinary speech “primary written authority” may mean that no other book exists, that every later commentary is thereby certified, that the church as church is infallible because it handles a book, that the investigation is finished, that private experience has been abolished, that every interpretation is as binding as the text, or that this chapter is already the First Principles. None of those senses is this chapter’s object.

Four elements belong to the definition.

First, **authority as standing to measure**. The object is not prestige, antiquity, sales, institutional endorsement, or the feeling that a volume is sacred. The object is standing: this writing binds as the decisive written court by which later written and spoken claims about truth are to be known, tested, reasoned through, taught, discerned, and lived.

Second, **written as the relevant class**. The object is not the claim that God has never spoken except in a book, and not the claim that no other writing of any kind exists. The object is firstness among written authorities. Other writings exist. They do not, by existing, become the measure of this writing.

Third, **primary as first among that class, not as the end of inquiry**. “Primary” names rank. It does not name the non-existence of secondary writings, the infallibility of readers, or the close of the book. Rank is the object. Closure of the investigation is not the object.

Fourth, **not those other claims**. The definition excludes, as not this object: a retry of Chapter 64; a rewrite of Chapter 58; a grant that every later interpreter’s claim is authoritative; a blank check for tradition or institution; the claim that the book is finished; Movement IX’s dedicated mishandling-file; Movement X’s labour-file; the First Principles as this file’s achievement. Those exclusions are part of the definition. They are not a later apology.

Thus:

> **The question of this chapter is not whether the Father’s word is truth, why the scripture cannot be broken, or how later readers mishandle an authoritative text. Those questions have been examined or remain later. The question is why the Graphē is the primary written authority in the sense just defined.**

# 65.7 Define “Authority” at This Stage

**Authority**, in this argument, is standing to govern judgment: the right of a source to bind the operations of knowing, testing, reasoning, teaching, discerning, and living, not as prestige and not as force of habit.

Four notes belong to the definition at this stage.

First, **standing, not volume of commentary**. A writing does not become authoritative by attracting interpreters. Interpreters become answerable because the writing already stands.

The present file does not convert recoverability of the text into a claim that every later sheet is the autograph, and it does not convert standing of the writing into standing of the reader.

Fourth, **at this level, as primary measure**. The standing here is the standing of the Graphē as the decisive written court among written authorities. Chapter 58 named the object and the standing already earned, and refused to draw this gate. The gate is now the object.

Thus:

> **Authority, at this stage, is the Graphē’s standing to govern judgment as God’s identified, authenticated, inspired, unbreakable, truthful writing. Prestige is not the definition. Later office is not the origin. The reader is not the seat.**

# 65.8 Define “Written” at This Stage

**Written**, in this argument, names the class in which the Graphē stands as measure: Holy Writ as γραφή — writing that, in biblical usage, designates Scripture — not speech in general, not inward impression, and not every later document that happens to be inked.

Chapters 20–22 already established the lexical field. γραφή (*graphē*, G1124) belongs first to writing / a written thing. Biblical usage extends the term to Holy Writ / Scripture. “Word of God” is a broader category whose particular referent is determined by context. Scripture is the authoritative written category within that field. Those conclusions remain premises. The present file uses them.

Why “written” matters to the gate is not a preference for books over speech. God has spoken audibly; angels have mediated; prophets have spoken; Christ has spoken; apostles have spoken; visions and dreams have been given (Chapters 23–28, used not retried). The investigation has already followed those modes into written testimony (Chapter 32). The gate does not cancel speech. It locates the abiding public measure among writings. What is written can be cited as court, searched as already standing, received as not the word of men, and appealed to as “it is written.” What is only presently felt cannot, by inwardness, occupy that office.

The Dedication controversy exhibited the point. He did not ask what His opponents presently felt about a man who makes Himself God. He asked whether it is not written in their law. The court was the writing. Chapter 61 already showed that weight. The present file uses it: primary *written* authority is not a slogan against all speech; it is the rank of this writing as the measure that remains when speakers disagree.

Thus:

> **“Written” names the class of the measure. The Graphē is Holy Writ. Other writings exist. Inward impressions occur. Neither, by existing or occurring, becomes this writing.**

# 65.9 Define “Primary” at This Stage

**Primary**, in this argument, names firstness of rank among written authorities for the operations the locked assignment lists. It does not name sole existence. It does not name chronological firstness as if later apostolic writings were secondary in standing. It does not name the reader’s first impression. It names the writing that stands as decisive measure when written authorities conflict.

Three clarifications belong to the word.

First, **primary among written authorities, not primary instead of God**. God is the ground of truth (Chapter 10). The Graphē is His writing. Rank among writings is not rivalry with the Speaker. A measure that displaced God would not be His writing. A piety that invoked God in order to evade His writing would not be obedience to the Speaker the writings present.

Second, **primary as measure, not primary as the only voice God has ever used**. The modes of giving remain in force. Primary written authority is the rank of the resulting Holy Writ among writings that claim to govern truth.

Third, **primary as first court, not as the claim that secondary writings are worthless**. Lexicons, histories, commentaries, and later books may illuminate. They do not, by illuminating, become the court. Chapter 14 already required that human accounts about God be tested. The present definition states the court by which they are tested as writings: the Graphē.

The locked assignment names the operations: known, tested, reasoned through, taught, discerned, and lived. “Primary” means that, for those operations, this writing is the decisive written measure. A later system may assist. It may not replace.

Thus:

> **“Primary” is firstness of written measure. It is not atheism of other books, not rivalry with God, not chronological slighting of later Scripture, and not a compliment paid to a favourite volume.**

# 65.10 What “Primary” Does Not Mean

The word is now defined by exclusion as well as by rank, so that the positive conclusion cannot be inflated by a slogan.

**It does not mean that no other writing exists.** Histories exist. Commentaries exist. Letters exist. Later religious books exist. Their existence is a fact. Their rank is the question. Primary written authority answers the question of rank. It does not erase the fact.

**It does not mean that interpreters are infallible.** The writing stands. The reader may err. Chapter 58 already named the distinction. Movement IX will examine it as a dedicated problem. The present file foreshadows the distinction as a limit of the gate, not as that later file.

**It does not mean that tradition or institution is a second Graphē, or a higher court above it.** Recognition receives what already stands. Office may serve the writing. Office does not, by serving, become the measure of the writing.

**It does not mean that private experience has been abolished.** The writings themselves speak of the Spirit, of the word working in those who believe, of conviction, of knowledge. None of those operations is identical with making experience the court of the writing. Experience is not, by intensity, the Graphē.

**It does not mean that the book is finished.** Movement VIII closes. The investigation continues.

**It does not mean that every later claim about the text is interchangeable with the text.** Quotation is not the writing. Commentary is not the writing. A slogan that uses the writing’s words is not, by using them, the writing.

**It does not mean that the First Principles have been taught, or that *spoudazō* has been examined.** Those remain later. The gate names why later labour has an object worthy of labour. It does not perform the labour.

Thus:

> **The exclusions are the shape of “primary.” They are not a withdrawal of the rank. The Graphē is the decisive written measure. Other writings, readers, traditions, and experiences remain what they are: not that measure.**

# 65.11 The Graphē Named as the Object

The object of the gate is the Graphē: γραφή (*graphē*, G1124), writing that, in biblical usage, designates Holy Writ / Scripture — the scriptural corpus already identified as the body of writings the biblical record itself treats as Scripture, earlier and later, recognized rather than invented, grounded in prophetic and apostolic commission, one written testimony, God-breathed, non-failing as record of what it actually communicates, standing as the written Word.

Chapters 20–22 named the term. Chapters 51–58 identified the body and the standing. Chapters 59–64 exhibited the Truth’s verdict upon that writing. The present file does not re-open those files. It names the same object as the primary written authority.

The naming refuses two flattenings.

**Flattening downward.** “Graphē” is not a pious synonym for “religious literature.” Not every writing about God is Holy Writ. Not every later claimant of inspiration deposits Scripture.

**Flattening upward.** “Graphē” is not a synonym for God, for the Son as the Word in John 1:1, or for every occurrence of λόγος. Chapter 22 and Chapter 64 already forbade those collapses. The Graphē is the Father’s word as writing, already identified. The Son is the Truth who treats that writing as court and who identifies the Father’s word as truth. Distinction preserves both.

Thus:

> **The gate attaches to the Graphē so identified. It does not attach to an undefined “Bible-feeling,” to every later book, or to the interpreter as interpreter.**

# 65.12 The Six Functions: Known, Tested, Reasoned Through, Taught, Discerned, and Lived

The locked assignment does not say only that the Graphē is primary. It names the operations for which it is primary. Those operations are not decoration. They are the work the writing governs.

**Known.** Truth is known, in this investigation, by evidence, testimony, revelation, and reason (Chapters 4–7, 13). The Graphē is the primary written means by which what God has made known is available as public writing. Knowledge is not reduced to quotation. Quotation of an authoritative text can convey genuine knowledge; possession of words is not yet understanding of meaning — a distinction Movement X will examine. The present file names the writing as the primary written source of that knowledge. It does not write the quotation-file.

**Tested.** Claims are tested by a measure. Isaiah 8:20 already exhibited the function: “To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.” Acts 17:11 exhibited the same function in another scene: they searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so. Testing is not suspicion as a mood. It is the operation Movement I required: a claim is not true because it is claimed. The Graphē is the primary written court of the test.

**Reasoned through.** Isaiah 1:18 remains in force: “Come now, and let us reason together.” Chapter 7 already established that engaging the Word requires intellectual engagement. The present file does not rewrite that chapter. It locates the writing as the primary written material through which that reasoning is to be conducted. Reason is not a rival court above the Graphē. Reason is the inspectable operation by which the Graphē is followed as argument rather than as noise.

**Taught.** 2 Timothy 3:16 remains in force: Scripture is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness. Teaching that will not sit under the writing is not, by being called teaching, the measure of the writing. The Graphē is the primary written curriculum. Later teachers may serve it. They do not replace it.

**Discerned.** Discernment distinguishes truth from error (Chapter 3). The writing supplies the primary written standard of that distinction. Hebrews 5:14 will later name senses exercised to discern both good and evil. The present file does not write that later maturity-file. It names the writing as the standard without which “discernment” collapses into taste.

**Lived.** John 17:17 already joined the Father’s word as truth to sanctification through that truth. John 8:31–32 joined continuance in His word to knowing the truth and being made free. James 1:22 commands doing, not hearing only. Living is not a second source that judges the writing. Living is the life the writing governs. The reconstructible proposition that experience in conformity with Christ is governed by the Graphē’s truth belongs here as coherence, not as “the source says,” and not as a licence that baptizes every later practice.

The six functions are one office described six ways. They are not six independent proofs. A writing that is truth, unbreakable because of what it is, treated as court by the Truth, God-breathed of the God who cannot lie, is the writing by which those operations are to be conducted as written operations.

Thus:

> **Primary written authority is authority for these operations. The list is the locked assignment’s own account of the office. It is not a later ethics-book, and it is not a labour-file written early.**

# 65.13 Positive Warrant: The God Who Cannot Lie

The first member of the join is already in force. God is the ground of truth. He is truthful, faithful, and immutable. Falsehood is incompatible with Him. Chapters 10–12 established those conclusions. This chapter does not retry them. It uses them.

If the Graphē is His writing — and Movement VII has already warranted that identification at the level earned — then the writing is not His lie. A lie cannot be the primary written measure of truth. The writing of the God who cannot lie can be that measure, once it has been authenticated as His, inspired as writing, identified as this corpus, and treated as court by the Truth.

The member is source-side. It does not, by itself, identify which writings are His. Later members do that work. The member does this work: once those writings are so identified, their rank among written authorities is not a compliment paid by later readers. It is the consequence of the Speaker’s character.

A competing piety says that because God cannot lie, whatever a later speaker sincerely attributes to God is thereby true. Chapter 3 already refused sincerity as a determinant of truth. Chapter 14 already required that human accounts about God be tested. The present member does not baptize later speech. It grounds the writing already identified.

Thus:

> **The God who cannot lie is the source-side ground of the gate. The member is used, not retried. It does not, by itself, finish the join, and the join cannot be finished without it.**

# 65.14 Positive Warrant: Revelation Given and Authenticated

**Shortcut of mere claim.** “The Bible is God’s Word because the Bible says it is God’s Word” remains forbidden as a circle (methodology; Chapter 58’s anti-circularity). Self-testimony is evidence within the cumulative case. It is not a licence to bypass authentication.

**Shortcut of mere institution.** A later body may receive. Reception is not, by itself, the authentication-file already performed.

# 65.15 Positive Warrant: God-Breathed Writing

# 65.16 Positive Warrant: Corpus, Recognition, and Authority Already Earned

The fourth member is already in force. The scriptural corpus is the body of writings the biblical record itself treats as Scripture (Chapter 51). Canon is recognition of writings that already stand, not a later council inventing authority by vote (Chapter 52). Prophetic and apostolic authority is the commissioned standing under which those writings are received rather than invented (Chapter 53). The witnesses so identified constitute one written testimony (Chapter 54). Revelation, inspiration, and canon remain distinct operations (Chapter 55). The gate uses the member in a specific way. Primary written authority is not an undefined reverence for “the Bible” as a cultural object. It attaches to this body: earlier written witnesses already standing as Holy Writ, later written witnesses continuous with that body, received rather than invented. A later list may evidence recognition. A later list does not create the standing now ranked as primary.

The member refuses two rival stories.

**Story of later invention.** If a later vote created Scripture, then a later vote could uncreate it, and primary written authority would be a temporary institutional mood. Chapter 52 has already refused the story as canon-doctrine. The present file refuses it as rank-doctrine.

**Story of two rival Bibles.** If later apostolic writings were a second library in competition with the Scriptures Christ confirmed, then “primary” would name a choice between corpora. Chapter 50 and Chapter 54 have already refused the rivalry. The present file inherits one testimony.

> **Corpus, recognition, and already-earned written standing are the fourth member. The gate is not a mood about an undefined book. It is rank for this identified body. The member is used, not retried.**

# 65.17 Positive Warrant: Christ’s Appeal to What Is Written

The proposal fails as an account of His practice.

# 65.18 Positive Warrant: Cannot Be Broken Because of What It Is

The sixth member is already in force. οὐ δύναται λυθῆναι ἡ γραφή is Christ’s assertion of the Graphē’s standing in the court He has opened (Chapter 62). The scripture cannot be broken because of what it is: God’s truthful written Word, God-breathed, non-failing as record of what it actually communicates, standing as that writing, such that its force as court cannot be loosed without denying what it is (Chapter 63). His saying it is the assertion — the Truth’s verdict. The why is the doctrine of the writing. Those conclusions remain premises.

The gate uses the member in a specific way. A writing that cannot be loosed as court is not one exhibit among equals. Equals can be set aside when a more convenient exhibit appears. The Graphē cannot be set aside so as to evacuate the appeal. Primary written authority is the rank that unloosenability warrants among written measures. If a later tradition, institution, or experience could loose the writing’s force, the writing would not be what Chapters 62–63 have shown it to be.

The member does not say that every later copy is physically indestructible, or that every interpreter is unbreakable. Chapter 62 already refused those transfers. The present file inherits the refusal. Unloosenable writing is not unbreakable readers.

Thus:

> **Unloosenability because of what the writing is is the sixth member. A court that cannot be loosed is not one written authority among interchangeable others. The member is used, not retried.**

# 65.19 Positive Warrant: Thy Word Is Truth

The seventh member is already in force. John 17:17 identifies the Father’s word as truth. Sanctification through truth is through that word because that word is truth (Chapter 64). The identification coheres with Movements I–II and with the why of unloosenability. Those conclusions remain premises.

The gate uses the member in a specific way. A writing that is the Father’s word written, and a word that is truth, is not a mixed container from which later courts may select a remainder as higher truth. Identification forbids containment as the primary sense. Identification also forbids usefulness of a fallible remainder as the identity of the word. The writing that is that word as Graphē is therefore the primary written measure of truth. Later writings may be true insofar as they conform to it. They are not, by being writings, its measure.

The member does not say that every later interpreter’s claim is truth. Chapter 64 already refused that transfer. The present file inherits the refusal as a limit of the gate.

Thus:

> **“Thy word is truth” is the seventh member. A word that is truth, written as Graphē, is the decisive written measure of truth. The member is used, not retried.**

# 65.20 The Join: Why These Yield Primary Written Authority

The seven members are not a heap. They are a conjunction.

If God could lie, His writing would not be a measure of truth. He cannot.

If He had not made known, or if the claimed making-known had not been authenticated, the writing would be an unexamined religious book. He has made known. The claim has been authenticated at the level earned.

If Scripture were not God-breathed as writing, or if “all” were quietly weakened, or if the record failed as truthful divine writing of what it actually communicates, the writing would not bind as God’s writing. It is God-breathed. “All” stands. The record does not fail as record of what it actually communicates.

If the corpus were an undefined pile, or a later vote, or two rival libraries, “the Graphē” would not name an identified body. It names that body, recognized rather than invented, one testimony.

If the Truth had treated the writing as decoration, later courts could claim His practice as their warrant for sitting above it. He treated it as court.

If the writing could be loosed as court, later authorities could evacuate it when inconvenient. It cannot be loosed, because of what it is.

If the Father’s word were only a container of selected truths, or a useful fallible instrument, later writings could supply the “higher” remainder. The Father’s word is truth.

Therefore the Graphē is the primary written authority by which truth is known, tested, reasoned through, taught, discerned, and lived.

The inference is not: the Bible is primary because the Bible says it is primary. The inference is: the Speaker cannot lie; He has made known; the making-known has been authenticated; the writing is God-breathed and does not fail as record; this is the corpus; the Truth has used it as court; it cannot be loosed because of what it is; that word is truth; therefore this writing is the decisive written measure.

Each member has been established in its file. The present file performs the join. A join is not a digest of those files. A join is the inspectable consequence they jointly warrant.

Thus:

> **The gate is the conjunction. Remove a member and the rank is no longer the rank the investigation has earned. Keep the members and the rank follows with full force.**

The form does not, in this chapter, become a catalogue of every “it is written” in the corpus. The form is sufficient as record: the writings themselves treat writing as the court to which speakers appeal and by which speakers are tested.

# 65.28 Reconstructing the Aletheia Proposition: Conformity Pillows in the Graphē

A reconstructible proposition from the project’s own doctrinal materials states that true experience in conformity with Christ pillows in the unleavened sincerity and truth of the Graphē — Holy Writ as writings of unchallenged standing — whether for persons, organizations, governments, or nations; and that this writing is the premiere convention for hearing from God, as distinct from analogical nonliteral religious substitutes.

**Premises.** The Father’s word is truth (Chapter 64). The writing cannot be broken because of what it is (Chapter 63). The Truth treats the writing as court (Chapters 59–61). The writing is God-breathed Holy Writ of the God who cannot lie (Chapters 10–12, 30–31, 51–58).

**What follows.** The proposition coheres with the gate: primary written authority is the standing such experience requires. Organizations and nations are not excluded from the measure by being large. Labels are not the measure.

**What does not follow here.** That the First Principles have been taught. That *apeiros* and *spoudazō* have been examined. That every later practice calling itself Christian is thereby certified. That sincerity is truth. Those remain later or already refused.

Thus:

> **The reconstructible proposition is received as coherence with the gate, not as a substitute for the join, and not as Movement IX or the First Principles written now.**

# 65.29 Reconstructing the Bible-Introduction Proposition: What the Writing Is as Measure
