What defines the work

Mission

To deliver Mathēteuō — Truth Education that enrolls the world as scholars: individuals, institutions, and governments learning and operating in Elohim; complete in the divine identity.

Mission defines Aletheia World. The organization exists to deliver Mathēteuō — not a louder feed, and not a set of disconnected sites.

Aletheia is the study platform of that organization: courses, a book reader, a shop, and the rest of the study materials. Foundation School (a year of first-principles courses), the books from Aletheia Publications, and the study materials for that year live there.
