The entrance

The Graphē gate

Before doctrine can be tested, taught, or lived, the authority for truth must be settled. That authority is the γραφή (graphē): Holy Writ, the written Word. The gate is not one of the six first principles. It establishes the court by which they are read.

One Source of Truth — The Doctrine of the Graphē is the gate book and course. It is included with the year.

The handbook

The Principles of the Doctrine of Christ

A concise reference on the oracles and the six first principles. It is included with The Walk and The Intensive. It supports the courses; it does not replace them. A student should not need to leave a lesson before the doctrine becomes intelligible.
