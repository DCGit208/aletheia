Individuals. Institutions. Governments. One charge — to learn and operate in Elohim, complete in the divine identity.

I

Individuals

The person

You are not a consumer of content. You are a scholar of life in Elohim — to learn to operate in the completeness of their divine identity. Colossians 2:10. In Aletheia you get Foundation School (a year of first-principles courses), the books from Aletheia Publications, and the study materials for that year.

II

Institutions

The body

Churches, organizations, and schools that will not leave their people to relative truth. Mathēteuō is Truth Education they can carry together — shared teaching, books, and one study platform — so the body learns and operates in Elohim as one. John 17:20–23.

III

Governments

The people under authority

Authority is not excused from scholarship. Governments are enrolled in the same charge: learning and operating in Elohim with regard to household and national life — veracity over ideology, identity over noise.
