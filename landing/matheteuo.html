Mission

To deliver Mathēteuō — Truth Education that enrolls the world as scholars: individuals, institutions, and governments learning and operating in Elohim; complete in the divine identity.

Enrolled as scholars

The person · the body · the people under authority

I

Individuals

The person

You are not a consumer of content. You are a scholar of life in Elohim — to learn to operate in the completeness of their divine identity. Colossians 2:10. In Aletheia you get Foundation School (a year of first-principles courses), the books from Aletheia Publications, and the study materials for that year.

II

Institutions

The body

Churches, organizations, and schools that will not leave their people to relative truth. Mathēteuō is Truth Education they can carry together — shared teaching, books, and one study platform — so the body learns and operates in Elohim as one. John 17:20–23.

III

Governments

The people under authority

Authority is not excused from scholarship. Governments are enrolled in the same charge: learning and operating in Elohim with regard to household and national life — veracity over ideology, identity over noise.

How the work is done

What Aletheia World provides

  1. I
    Foundation School A year of first-principles courses: the Graphē gate (the written Word as the authority for truth), the handbook, and the six first principles. You take the year in Aletheia.
  2. II
    Aletheia Publications The books that serve Mathēteuō. You read and buy them in Aletheia.
  3. III
    Aletheia The study platform: courses, a book reader, a shop, and the rest of the study materials.
  4. IV
    Partnership Churches, organizations, schools, and governments who will carry Mathēteuō with their people — shared teaching, books, and one study platform.
