# 07 — Distinctions that must not be lost

These are **structure**, not colour. If a lesson flattens one of them, it has left the source.

## Opening

1. Naturalistic Christian experience (conformity to the graphē) vs ersatz analogical religiousisms.
2. First principles vs maturity (perfection). The foundation is laid, then you go on — you do not skip it, and you do not relaid it as a lifestyle.
3. Milk vs strong meat.
4. Babe (nēpios, apeiros, dull of hearing) vs teacher whose senses are exercised.
5. Knowledge as perception + learning + **reasoning** vs information collected.

## Repentance

6. Change over **sin and its cause** vs change over **consequence** (Hell) alone.
7. **Metanoia** (G3341) vs **metamellomai** (G3338): real change toward sin and its cause vs regret of consequences only (Judas).
8. Metanoia includes **reformation / new creature**, not remorse only.
9. Two old things: **old man** (devil’s spirit and nature in the disobedient) vs **outward sin**.
10. Christ the only way vs any other name or rite.
11. Salvation from **sin** as the salvation needed vs other “salvations” treated as the main thing.

## Faith

12. Pistis as **state / sonship / adoption** vs faith as a mood.
13. Image and likeness (true nature) vs the glory come short through sin. Teach Genesis 1:26-28 as written.
14. Justification **free** and **present** (ransom in full) vs innocence earned later.
15. Heir / son vs servant / slave (carries into Course 3).

## Baptisms

16. **Seven in Scripture** (owner B2) vs **three for believers**. Do not add an eighth baptism. Do not pretend there are only three in the Bible.
17. Baptism **into Christ** (saves the soul; “one baptism”) vs **water** (sign after salvation) vs **Spirit** (sonship witness **and** enduement of power).
18. Three agents: Spirit into Christ; Christ in the Spirit; minister in water.
19. Sign vs object. Water points; it does not save.
20. Preaching of the cross as power vs water as power.
21. Spirit **without measure** / fullness vs a stingy “second blessing” slogan that ignores the source’s sonship-prerequisite.
22. Son (Abba, heir, informant Spirit) vs slave (timidity). You are not given a spirit of fear. The heir is the fear and dread (Genesis 9:2; Deuteronomy 11:25).

## Hands

21. Nine occasions — each its own text — vs one vague “immissions” practice.
22. **Resultant** vs **foundation**. Hands do not substitute for principles 1–3.

## Resurrection and chronology

23. **Spiritual** resurrection vs **material** resurrection.
24. Spiritual resurrection **in this life only** vs any offer of new birth after death.
25. Material = **body**, not spirit.
26. Resurrection of **life** (before Millennium) vs resurrection of **damnation** (after).
27. **Temporary** raising vs **permanent** / immortal body.
28. **Five companies / raptures** in the first resurrection vs one undifferentiated “the rapture.”
29. **Rapture 2** vs **second advent** (with the saints, after tribulation).
30. First resurrection (rule; second death has no power) vs the raising of those **without Christ** after the Millennium.

## Judgment and the underworld

31. Three deaths: physical / spiritual / second.
32. Death as cutting off from Genesis 1:26-28 purpose vs “dead” as a single slang.
33. Soul and spirit **immortal** vs resurrection-as-spirit. Bodies die and rise.
34. **Five prisons**, none identical with the **grave**.
35. Tartarus (pre-flood fallen angels only) vs Paradise (once righteous, now empty) vs Hell/sheol-hades (wicked until Millennium’s end) vs abyss/Abaddon (demons / some angels; Satan bound in the 1000 years) vs lake of fire/gehenna (final, eternal).
36. Book of life as a book from which names **can be blotted** vs a slogan that nothing can be lost.

## Books

37. *Spirit Witness* vs *Presence of Sons* (series 1–4).
38. *Why We’re Beyond* vs *Fearfully Made*.
39. In-body first-wave titles vs back-list titles (not Year-1 courses).
40. Book vs course vs school program vs entitlement.
