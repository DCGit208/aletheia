# 06 — Source glossary

Definitions follow **the leaflet’s use**. Where a lexicon would add a seminary list the leaflet does not use, that list is omitted.

| Term | Language | Source use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| graphē | Gk. | Holy Writ: Bible, Scripture. Unchallenged authority (John 10:35). The experience pillows here, not in nonliteral religiousisms. |
| oracles of God | — | Hebrews 5:12. The first principles are the cornerstone. |
| milk / strong meat | — | Diet of babes vs the mature (Hebrews 5:12-14). |
| apeiros | Gk. | Unskilful: inexperienced, ignorant — in the **word of righteousness** (Hebrews 5:13). |
| nēpios | Gk. | Babe: simple-minded, immature Christian. Two marks: dull of hearing; unskilled in the Word (basis of salvation). |
| spoudazō | Gk. | Diligence, speed, effort, labour, **study intellectually** (2 Timothy 2:15). |
| knowledge | — | Mental result of perception, learning, and **reasoning**. Word exercise / word drill. |
| maturity / perfection | — | Second movement after first principles. Banner of the leaflet. |
| metanoia | Gk. | One of eight original words for repentance (others not listed). Compunction for guilt **including reformation**: regeneration, quickening, recreation, new creature. Change of mind over sin **and the cause**. Also: reversal of another’s decision. |
| nekros ergon / nekra erga | Gk. | Dead works. Works of the old man — spirit and nature of the devil; old nature (trespasses and sins). Printed also `negra erga`. |
| trespasses | — | Deviation from moral equity (source gloss on Ephesians 2:1). |
| old man | — | Spirit, nature, and power of sin; **nothing more nor less than** the spirit and nature of the devil working in the disobedient. |
| outward sin | — | The second old thing (works of the flesh, transgression, lust’s child, what proceeds from the heart, Romans 1 catalogue). |
| Hell (as consequence) | — | Named as the consequence of sin, not the definition of repentance. Final: lake of fire. |
| pistis | Gk. | Credence. Defines our **state as children of God**; acceptance and adoption as sons. |
| ’ĕlōhîm | Heb. | God; source: plural, Trinity (Genesis 1:26). |
| image / likeness | — | Genesis 1:26-27. Man made like his Maker. True nature. The glory come short. |
| dikaiosynē | Gk. | Righteousness / justification. Equity: impartiality to and **conformity with the nature of God**; original, back on course. You are this in Christ (2 Cor 5:21). |
| dikaioō | Gk. | Justify: render just or innocent (free from evil or guilt). Done **freely**. |
| apolutrōsis | Gk. | Redemption: ransom **in full**. |
| heir | — | Source asks the meaning; points to Galatians 4:5-7 (son, not servant; heir of God through Christ). |
| baptismos | Gk. | Ablution: ritual washing. The Word’s washing at new birth. |
| baptizō | Gk. | To make whelmed — fully wet, overpowered, submerged. Used of into-Christ, water, and (by implication) Spirit without measure. |
| one baptism | — | Ephesians 4:5. Baptism into Christ and His body. **Only this saves the soul.** |
| water baptism | — | After one is saved. Figure/sign of death, burial, resurrection. Does not save. |
| Spirit baptism | — | Witness (informant) of sonship; hereditary; without measure; fullness. Prerequisite: new birth. |
| anangellō | Gk. | Shall shew: announce in detail; declare, rehearse, report. John 16:14. |
| without measure | — | John 3:34 / 7:38 as the source applies it: the measure Christ experienced; the Spirit not by measure **unto you**. |
| laying on of hands | — | Nine occasions. Resultant, not substantively the foundation. |
| spiritual resurrection | — | New birth; quickened from death in trespasses. **This life only.** |
| material resurrection | — | Resurrection of the **body**, not the spirit. Two issues, 1000 years apart. |
| first resurrection | — | The righteous to life before the Millennium. Covers **five raptures / companies**. Those who have part in it rule with Christ. Second death has no power (Rev 20:6). |
| rapture 2 | — | Company 2: they that are Christ’s at His coming. **Not** the second advent. Tribulation is 7 years after this. |
| tribulation | — | Seven years after rapture 2. Rev 6–19 in the source’s span. |
| second advent | — | Christ **with the saints** after the tribulation; ushers the Millennium. |
| Millennium | — | One thousand years **after** the tribulation. First-resurrection saints rule. Wicked not yet raised. |
| temporary resurrection | — | e.g. Matthew 9:25. Not yet immortality. |
| permanent resurrection | — | Raised to immortality. Christ the firstfruits. Mt 27 saints now in heaven with bodies. |
| book of life | — | Names may be blotted. Whoever is not found written is cast into the lake of fire. |
| death | — | Predominantly: separation / cutting off from realizing Genesis 1:26-28 purpose. Three kinds. |
| physical death | — | Inner man (soul and spirit) separated from the body (James 2:26). |
| spiritual death | — | Man separated from God because of sin. |
| second death | — | Eternal separation from God in the lake of fire. |
| immortal soul/spirit | — | Soul and spirit do not die. Resurrection texts concern **bodies**. |
| Tartarus | Gk. | Prison of fallen angels who sinned before the flood. No humans. No demons. |
| Paradise | — | Former abode of the righteous at death. **Now empty** of the righteous. They go to heaven since Christ’s ascension. |
| Hell / sheol-hades | — | Torment compartment for the wicked until the end of the Millennium; then emptied into the lake. |
| abyss / bottomless pit / Abaddon | — | Abode of demons and some angelic beings. Abaddon = destruction. Satan bound here 1000 years. |
| lake of fire / gehenna | — | Final, eternal hell of devil, angels, demons, wicked men. |
| FTH RD AV | — | Further reading available. In-body titles are first-wave companions; the back page is the wider list. |

**Not in this glossary (not supplied by the source):** the other seven “original words” for repentance; the unnamed four of seven baptisms; a systematic lexicon of every NT word for faith or baptism.
