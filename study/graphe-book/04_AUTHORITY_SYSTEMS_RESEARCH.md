**Purpose:** Internal file for Question One. Not reader-facing.

**Method for every case (when chapters are written):**

5. No caricature. No ridicule. Not a people-attack.

## Catholic structure (accurate sketch — verify quotes when writing)

Official teaching does **not** say “three equal Bibles.” It says Sacred Scripture and Sacred Tradition make up a single sacred deposit of the Word of God, and the task of authentically interpreting the Word of God is entrusted to the living Magisterium. The three are described as so connected that one cannot stand without the others (*Dei Verbum* 10; CCC 80–85, 95).

**Graphē question (not a slur):** When the authentic interpreter and the writing appear to conflict in the believer’s hearing, **who is the final court?** Does the writing remain independently sufficient to test the interpreter?
