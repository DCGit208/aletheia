# 05 — Graphē Argument Map

## The book’s two questions

1. **What is the final authority for truth?**  
2. **What happens to a person or institution when that authority is not known, handled, reasoned through, or obeyed?**

Thesis: There is one authoritative written source by which Christian truth is to be known, examined, reasoned through, established, taught, discerned, and lived: the graphē — Holy Writ.

## Progressive argument (17 steps — working)

1. Truth requires authority.  
2. Competing authorities require a standard that can judge them.  
3. An authority cannot merely authenticate itself by assertion.  
4. Persons, organizations, governments, and nations are already under Holy Writ (manuscript).  
5. Age-old ostensibly Christianized organizations are not exempt (manuscript).  
6. Interpretation exists; the interpreter may stand under, beside, or above the writing.  
7. When the interpreter is the final court, the believer cannot test doctrine except by that interpreter.  
8. John 10:35: the scripture cannot be broken; Holy Writ is unchallenged-unquestionable authority.  
9. The graphē is the premiere convention for hearing from God and spiritual growth.  
10. Growth is first principles → maturity (perfection).  
11. Knowledge is perception + learning + reasoning; practice is Word exercise / Word drill.  
12. That knowledge requires spoudazō — intellectual labour (2 Tim 2:15).  
13. Absence of reasonability + apeiros → dull hearing / nēpios → aridity → tergiversation → heresies called doctrines.  
14. Therefore the authority question **is** the maturity question.  
15. The six first principles are the cornerstone content the oracles name — a test of the method, not a second book.  
16. Continue in His word → know the truth → the truth makes free (John 8:31-32).  
17. One source of truth: the Graphē.

## Positive chain (manuscript)

Graphē → Holy Writ → authority (imprimatur) → oracles / cornerstone → hearing & growth (premiere convention) → first principles → maturity → knowledge (perception, learning, reasoning) → Word exercise / drill → productive living / control / godliness → truth → freedom.

## Diagnostic chain (manuscript)

Absence of reasonability + unskillfulness (apeiros) → aridity + tergiversation (persons and organizations) → damnable accepted heresies called doctrines.  
Nēpios = unskilled in the word of righteousness. Two marks: dull of hearing; unskilled in the Word (basis for salvation).  
Blame: failure of scholarly impetus / failure to know the scriptures (any copy).

## What must not happen to the argument

- Reduce to “the Bible is important.”  
- Reduce to a Catholic attack.  
- Reduce to six doctrine summaries.  
- Treat this volume as the premiere convention.  
- Treat the Doctrine of the Graphē as a seventh first principle.
