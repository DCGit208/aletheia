## How to read a row

- **Loc** — page  
- **Wording** — manuscript proposition (compressed to the claim; unusual terms kept)  
- **Concept** — name of the idea  
- **L1** — Scripture the manuscript cites  
- **Term** — Greek/Hebrew the manuscript supplies  
- **Def** — definition the manuscript itself gives  
- **Links** — logical relation to surrounding claims  
- **Doct / Epist / Herm / Mat** — significance  
- **Ch** — working architecture chapter (see `07`)  
- **Action** — preserve / expand / clarify / research / companion / verify

## Movement A — recovered chains (not extra source)

**Positive (compressed in p.1):**  
Graphē → Holy Writ → imprimatur → (persons/orgs/governments/nations) → oracles / cornerstone → premiere convention (hearing + growth) → first principles → maturity → reasonability + skill → scholarly impetus → spoudazō → knowledge (perception + learning + reasoning) → Word exercise / Word drill → productive living / control / godliness → truth → freedom.

**Diagnostic (explicit causal sentence):**  
Absence of reasonability + presence of unskillfulness (apeiros) → aridity + tergiversation (persons and age-old ostensibly Christianized organizations) → damnable accepted heresies called doctrines.  
Blame: failure to realize scholarly impetus / failure to know the scriptures (any media).  
Condition: nēpios = unskilled in the word of righteousness; two marks: dull of hearing; unskilled in the Word (basis for salvation).  
Lack named: cognitive skill / knowledge as defined.

### B1 Repentance from dead works (p.1–2)

- Heading: Gr. **Metanoia** from **nekros ergon**.  
- Hebrews 6:1 quoted.  
- Metanoia: “one of the eight original words” — **OWNER B1:** teach **seven** words; no eighth invented.  
- Compunction for guilt including reformation; regeneration; quickening; recreation; New Creature (2 Cor 5:17); change of mind over **sin and the cause**, not merely consequence.  
- Printed consequence: `Mt.16:19-31`, Rev 20:10-15 — **OWNER A1:** Luke 16:19-31, not Matthew 16.  
- Also: reversal of another’s decision.  
- Nekros ergon / negra erga: works of the old man — spirit and nature of the devil (Eph 4:22); old nature (Eph 2:1); wages death (Rom 6:23).  
- Two old things that pass away: (1) spirit, nature, power of sin / old man / devil working in men of disobedience (Jn 8:44; Eph 2:2; 2 Cor 4:4; printed `1John 3:89, 5:18`); (2) outward sin (Gal 5:19-21 etc.).  
- Only way: Christ Jesus (printed Heb.914 = **9:14**; Eph 2:1-5); 2 Cor 5:17; Acts 4:12; receive Him: John 1:12; Rom 10:8-9.  
- Salvation from sin is the only salvation needed; sin the cause of death and depravity.  
- FTH RD AV: **THE POWER OF SALVATION**.  
- **Action:** Preserve direction; VERIFY printed citations; companion = Power of Salvation; in *this* book = test case of Graphē method.

### B2 Faith toward God (p.2)

- Hebrews 6:1. Gr. **pistis** = credence; acceptance (true nature); espousal/adoption as sons (Gal 4:5-7; Rom 8:15; Eph 1:5).  
- Oneness: Hb. **ʾĕlōhîm** plural / Trinity — Gen 1:26.  
- True nature from creation: Gen 1:26-27; 2 Pet 1:4; Ps 82:6; glory come short (Rom 3:23-24); 1 Cor 11:7.  
- Justified freely; **dikaioō** / **dikaiosynē**; **apolytrōsis**; 2 Cor 5:21; Heb 1:8; heir questions; John 17:20-23; 2 Cor 4:4, 6.  
- FTH RD AV: Doctrine of Man; Why Men Die; True Nature of God; Who’s Jesus to You?  
- **Action:** Preserve; companions as named; do not let Course 2 swallow this book.

### B3 Doctrine of baptisms (p.2–3)

- Printed heading “baptism” singular in one line; body: **baptismos**; three **essential** of **seven** mentioned. **OWNER:** seven baptisms / three agents — do not invent the unnamed four in Year-1.  
- Premiere of three: washing by the Word at new birth (Eph 5:26; 1 Pet 1:23; Jn 3:5).  
- 1. Baptism into Christ / body — one baptism that saves the soul (Eph 4:5; Gal 3:27; Rom 6:3-7; Col 2:12; 1 Cor 12:13). **baptizō** defined.  
- 2. Water baptizō after one is saved — symbol; 1 Pet 3:21; does not save; 1 Cor 1:17-24; Gal 3:26.  
- 3. Baptism in the Holy Spirit — witness of sonship; without measure (Jn 7:38; 3:34); fullness; Gal 4:6-7; 2 Tim 1:7; Jn 16:13-14; Gen 1:26-28.  
- Fear/dread charge: printed Jas 2:9 — **OWNER A5:** Gen 9:2; Deut 11:25.  
- FTH RD AV: Why We’re Beyond; Presence of Sons; Spirit Witness.  
- **Action:** Preserve three essential; companion bridge; VERIFY citations.

### B4 Laying on of hands (p.3)

- Nine occasions with verses: blessing men; offerings; ordaining; imparting Spirit; blessing children; healing; miracles; imparting gifts; imparting Spirit baptism.  
- Resultant, not substantive, until fundaments are actualized.  
- FTH RD AV. (no title). **OWNER:** no required companion.  
- **Action:** Preserve nine + “resultant”; short treatment in this book.

### B5 Resurrection of the dead (p.3–4)

- Two kinds: **spiritual** (new birth; this life only; Heb 9:27; Rev 22:11; drop Ecc 11:1 per OWNER A2) and **material** (body; Jn 5:28-29).  
- Two bodily issues a thousand years apart (Rev 20:4-6). Printed `Rev. 10:4-6, 11-15` — **OWNER A4:** teach Rev **20**.  
- Temporary vs permanent examples; Christ firstfruits; Mt 27:52-53.  
- Five companies / raptures in the first resurrection. Printed Rev 7:18 — **OWNER A3:** 7:1-8.  
- Chronology: tribulation 7 years after rapture 2; rapture 2 ≠ second advent; advent after tribulation ushers Millennium.  
- FTH RD AV: **MILLENNIUM**.  
- **Action:** Preserve system; do not collapse companies; companion Millennium; bounded here.

### B6 Eternal judgment (p.4–5)

- Eternal punishment (Mt 25:46; Rev 14:9-11; 20:11-15; Isa 66:22-24).  
- Book of life; second death; blotting names (Ex 32:32-33; Ps 69; Jn 3:36; Rev 3:5; 22:18-19).  
- Dead = separation from God’s purpose (Gen 1:26-28). Three deaths: physical, spiritual, second.  
- Soul/spirit immortal; resurrection is of bodies.  
- Five prisons, none the grave: Tartarus; Paradise (now empty of the righteous); Hell/sheol-hades; abyss/Abaddon; lake of fire/gehenna.  
- No in-body companion. Back-list on p.5.  
- **Action:** Preserve five prisons and three deaths; do not merge prisons with the grave.
