# 06 — Graphē Maturity Map

Maturity in the manuscript is **not** emotional adulthood. It is **First Principles → Maturity (Perfection)** under the premiere convention, requiring skill in the Word.

## Authority → maturity

| If… | Then maturity… |
| --- | --- |
| The writing is the final court | The believer can test claims; senses can be exercised (Heb 5:14 cluster) |
| An interpreter / tradition / office is the final court | Testing depends on that court; dull hearing can be institutionalized |
| Age or size authenticates doctrine | G10 already names age-old ostensibly Christianized organizations as able to go arid |
| Format (soft/hard copy) is treated as knowing | G12: failure to know remains |
| Quotation is treated as knowledge | G18: knowledge is perception + learning + reasoning |

## Manuscript marks of unreadiness

- Dull of hearing (Heb 5:11)  
- Need to be taught **again** the first principles (5:12)  
- Become such as have need of milk (5:12)  
- Apeiros in the word of righteousness (5:13)  
- Nēpios — person or organization (5:13)  
- Absence of reasonability  
- Aridity; tergiversation  
- Accepted heresies called doctrines  

## Manuscript marks of the path forward

- Premiere convention: hearing + spiritual growth  
- Scholarly impetus; align  
- Spoudazō — intellectually  
- Knowledge: perception, learning, reasoning  
- Word exercise / Word drill  
- First principles laid, then go on  
- Productive living; be in control; godliness  
- Truth makes free  

## Mandatory question at every major topic

How does this affect the ability to **hear**, **know**, **reason**, **discern**, **teach**, and **go on unto maturity**?
