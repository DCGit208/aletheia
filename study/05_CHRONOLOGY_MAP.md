# 05 — Eschatological chronology map

Studied from the leaflet as a **whole system**. This is a planning artifact. It is **not** a seventh course. Course 5 remains 9 weeks / ~18 lessons.

Do not reduce this to “rapture or tribulation first?”

---

## The source’s own clarifying sentence (page 4)

> The Millennium is the one thousand years period after the tribulation: the tribulation is 7 years after rapture 2, and rapture 2 is not Second Advent; 2nd advent (coming of Christ **with the saints**) is after Tribulation, ushering the Millennium: Rev. 6–19.

That sentence is the spine. The five companies hang on it. Revelation 20 hangs after it.

## Two kinds, then two material issues

| Kind | What rises | When | Texts the source uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spiritual | The person from death in trespasses (new birth) | **This life only**; forfeited after death if not born again | Eph 2:1-6; Eph 4:21-24; Col 3:10; 1 John 3:9; 1 Cor 15:19; Ecc 11:1; Heb 9:27; Rev 22:11 |
| Material | The **body**, not the spirit | Two issues, 1000 years apart | John 5:28-29; Rev 20:4-6, 10-15; Dan 12:2-3 |

| Material issue | Who | When | Destiny |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resurrection of life | The righteous / first resurrection companies | Before the Millennium (companies 1–5 complete the first resurrection) | Rule with Christ from the Millennium |
| Resurrection of damnation | The wicked, unsaved, unregenerate | After the Millennium | Lake of fire, forever |

---

## Rapture 2 vs second advent (must not collapse)

| | Rapture 2 (Company 2) | Second advent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source name | They that are Christ’s **at His coming** | Coming of Christ **with the saints** |
| Relation to tribulation | **Before**. Tribulation is 7 years **after** this. | **After** tribulation |
| Relation to Millennium | Does not start it | **Ushers** it |
| Sample texts | John 14:1-3 (receive you unto Myself); Luke 21:36 (escape); 1 Cor 15:51-54 | Rev 19:11-21; Rev 6–19 as the span |

Company 4 exists to make the “with the saints” advent possible: tribulation saints are still first-resurrection people, in time to return with Him.

---

## Temporary vs permanent (do not mix into the five companies)

- **Temporary:** Matthew 9:25 (and like raisings) — body lives again, not yet immortality.
- **Permanent:** immortal body. Christ the firstfruits of **all permanent** resurrections (1 Corinthians 15:1-23). Matthew 27:52-53 saints: source says they are **now in heaven with their bodies**.

---

## How Course 6 sits on this map

- During the present age: righteous who die go to **heaven** (Paradise empty since the ascension). Wicked go to **Hell / sheol-hades**.
- Tartarus already holds specified fallen angels (from before the flood).
- The abyss holds demons / some angels; Satan is bound there for the Millennium.
- At the great white throne **after** the Millennium: Hell emptied; bodies raised; lake of fire / gehenna is the **final** prison.

---

## What this map refuses

- One single “the rapture” that swallows companies 1–5.
- Second advent treated as rapture 2.
- Millennium treated as the last judgment.
- Wicked raised before the Millennium.
- New birth offered after death.
- A consultant rewrite into another eschatological school.
