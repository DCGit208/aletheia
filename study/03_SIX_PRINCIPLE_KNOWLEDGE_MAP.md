# 03 — Six-principle knowledge map

What a student must **know, distinguish, reason, and be able to teach** after each Year-1 course. Built from the leaflet, not from a generic systematic.

The school’s movement (Hebrews 5:11–6:3): first principles → knowledge → understanding → discernment → reasoning → application → ability to teach → maturity (perfection).

---

## Course 1 — Repentance from dead works (9 weeks)

**Governing line:** Metanoia is a real change of mind and attitude over **sin and its cause**, including reformation (new creature) — not remorse over Hell alone.

**Must know**

- Graphē is the imprimatur (John 10:35). The school begins here because babes are unskilled in the word of righteousness.
- Hebrews 5:11-14: ought to be teachers; milk / meat; apeiros; nēpios; two marks of babes (dull of hearing; unskilled in the Word).
- Spoudazō / 2 Timothy 2:15: study is labour. Knowledge = perception + learning + reasoning (word drill).
- Metanoia is one of eight original words for repentance. **Only this one is defined in the source.**
- Nekros ergon / nekra erga: works of the old man.
- Two old things that must pass (2 Corinthians 5:17): (1) spirit, nature, and power of sin — the old man = spirit and nature of the devil in the disobedient; (2) outward sin.
- Wages: death (Romans 6:23). Sin is the cause of all death and depravity.
- Only way: Christ — Hebrews 9:14 (conscience purged from dead works); 2 Corinthians 5:17; Acts 4:12.
- Receive Him: John 1:12; Romans 10:8-9.
- Salvation from **sin** is the salvation needed.

**Must distinguish**

- Cause vs consequence (Hell).
- Old man vs outward sin.
- Reformation / new creature vs feeling sorry.
- Milk vs meat; babe vs teacher.

**Must be able to teach**

- Why consequence-only “repentance” is not metanoia.
- Why Hebrews 9:14 belongs to this principle, not only to a later priesthood lecture.

**Companion:** *The Power of Salvation*.

## Course 2 — Faith toward God (9 weeks)

**Governing line:** Pistis (credence) defines our **state as children** — acceptance, adoption, oneness — on the basis of **true nature from creation**, restored freely in Christ.

**Must know**

- Pistis; adoption cluster (Galatians 4:5-7; Romans 8:15; Ephesians 1:5).
- ’Ĕlōhîm, plural, Trinity, Genesis 1:26-27 — image and likeness; “like his Maker.”
- 2 Peter 1:4; Psalm 82:6; 1 Corinthians 11:7 (man the express image and glory).
- Why Jesus came: Romans 3:23-24 — glory come short = deviation from true nature.
- Dikaioō; dikaiosynē (equity; conformity with the nature of God; back on course).
- Apolutrōsis: ransom **in full**. Justified **freely**. Present: you are the dikaiosynē of God (2 Corinthians 5:21).
- 2 Corinthians 4:4 and 4:6 — Christ the image; light in the face of Jesus Christ.

**Must distinguish**

- Faith as state/sonship vs faith as a feeling.
- Glory of God vs a vague “heaven someday.”
- Free justification vs earned innocence.

**Must reason (source questions)**

- Has man been justified? (Source answers: Yes, freely.)
- According to Hebrews 1:8, what does that make you?
- Who is heir of God? What does heir mean?
- How would you liken John 17:20-23?

**Companions:** *The Doctrine of Man* (already in the house); *Why Men Die*; *True Nature of God*; *Who’s Jesus to You?*

## Course 3 — The doctrine of baptisms (8 weeks)

**Governing line:** Three **essential** baptisms among **seven mentioned** in Scripture. Only baptism **into Christ** saves the soul. Water is a sign after one is saved. Spirit baptism is the **witness of sonship**, without measure.

**Must know**

- Baptismos (washing); baptizō (whelmed).
- Agency of the Word at new birth (Ephesians 5:26; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:5).
- Baptism 1: into Christ and His body at repentance and new birth — “one baptism” (Ephesians 4:5); Galatians 3:27; Romans 6:3-7; Colossians 2:12; 1 Corinthians 12:13.
- Baptism 2: water after salvation; figure of death, burial, resurrection (1 Peter 3:21); 1 Corinthians 1:17-24; Galatians 3:26.
- Baptism 3: Holy Spirit; Galatians 4:6-7; John 7:38; John 3:34 as the source uses “not by measure unto you”; fullness cluster; John 16:13-14; anangellō; Genesis 1:26-28 as “Mine.”
- Prerequisite of Spirit baptism: new birth.
- Not slaves; power, love, sound mind (2 Timothy 1:7); fear and dread cluster with *Why We’re Beyond*.

**Must distinguish**

- Saving baptism vs water sign vs Spirit witness.
- Sign vs object.
- Seven mentioned vs three essential (do not invent the unnamed four).

**Must answer (source question)**

- Is it the water that saves, or what it figures?

**Companions:** *Spirit Witness*; *Why We’re Beyond*.

## Course 4 — Laying on of hands (8 weeks)

**Governing line:** Nine biblical occasions. Resultant. Not a substitute for the foundation. Viable as the more grounding principles are actualized.

**Must know**

- Each of the nine texts as its own account (see matrix F1–F9).
- Hebrews 6:2 names this among the first principles **and** is cited again under imparting gifts.

**Must distinguish**

- Resultant practice vs foundation.
- Each occasion from the others (do not invent a tenth).

**Companion:** none named in the leaflet body.

---

## Course 5 — Resurrection of the dead (9 weeks)

**Governing line:** Two **kinds** (spiritual / material). Two **material** issues (life before the Millennium / damnation after). First material resurrection = **five companies / raptures**. Rapture 2 is **not** the second advent. Tribulation is **seven years after rapture 2**. Second advent is Christ **with the saints after** tribulation, ushering the Millennium.

**Must know**

- Spiritual resurrection = new birth now; only in this life; forfeited if not born again (cluster G3).
- Material = body, not spirit (John 5:28-29).
- 1000 years between the two bodily resurrections (Revelation 20:4-6).
- Temporary vs permanent; Christ the firstfruits; Matthew 27 saints now in heaven with bodies.
- Five companies (matrix H3–H7) and the chronology map.
- Facts-file (Job 19; Isaiah 26; Psalm 16:10; John 20–21; etc.).

**Must distinguish**

- Spiritual vs material.
- Temporary raising vs immortal body.
- Rapture 2 vs second advent.
- First resurrection (several companies) vs resurrection of the wicked after the Millennium.
- Company 1 (past) vs 2 (before tribulation) vs 3–5 (in tribulation).

**Must not do**

- Flatten five raptures into one.
- Ask only “rapture or tribulation first?”

**Companion:** *Millennium*.

---

## Course 6 — Eternal judgment (9 weeks)

**Governing line:** Eternal punishment; the book of life; three deaths; immortal soul/spirit and raised bodies; **five prisons**, none identical with the grave.

**Must know**

- Matthew 25:46 as eternal punishment; Revelation 20:11-15; book of life; second death; no respecter of persons.
- Blotting: Exodus 32; Psalm 69 (Judas); John 3:36; Revelation 3:5; 22:18-19.
- Death as cutting off from Genesis 1:26-28 purpose.
- Three deaths: physical (James 2:26); spiritual (Matthew 8:22; Colossians 2:14; 1 Timothy 5:6); second (Revelation 20:14 and parallels).
- Each prison: who is there, who is not, function, texts (matrix J5–J9).
- Paradise now empty of the righteous; they go to heaven at death since the ascension.

**Must distinguish**

- Three deaths.
- Five prisons vs “Hell” as a single room.
- Grave vs underworld.
- Sheol-hades (until end of Millennium) vs lake of fire / gehenna (eternal).

**Companion:** none named in the leaflet body.

## Relationships among the six (must not be lost)

```
Oracles / word-skill (Heb 5)
        ↓
1 Repentance (new creature; two old things; Christ the way)
        ↓
2 Faith (sonship, image, free justification, heir)
        ↓
3 Baptisms (into Christ at that new birth; water as sign; Spirit as witness of the son)
        ↓
4 Hands (resultant, after the grounding things)
        ↓
5 Resurrection (spiritual = that new birth in this life; material hope; companies)
        ↓
6 Judgment (deaths; prisons; book of life)
```

Course 3 depends on 1–2 (you cannot have Spirit witness of sonship without sonship). Course 4 says so explicitly. Course 5’s spiritual resurrection **is** Course 1’s new creature in another vocabulary. Course 6’s deaths interpret Course 1’s “wages.” Genesis 1:26-28 runs through Courses 2, 3, and 6.
