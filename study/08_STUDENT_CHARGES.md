# 08 — Student-charge map

Charges are **questions and imperatives the leaflet itself raises**. They are not slogans invented for a LMS.

A charge is finished when the student can answer **from the Scripture cluster**, not from a remembered heading.

---

## Assessment (v1 recommendation — owner still decides certificate rules)

These charges can serve as:

- Weekly written work (The Year: shorter; Intensive: longer)
- The spine of a **course examination** (one principle)
- Part of a **final** at week 52 (all six, with the chronology map and the five prisons)

Completion of activities is not automatically mastery. Certificate requirements remain an **owner** decision (`10_OWNER_QUESTIONS.md`).
