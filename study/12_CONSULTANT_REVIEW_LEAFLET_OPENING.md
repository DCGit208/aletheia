## 3. Draft of the teaching (consultant digest)

*Compressed from the authored chapter — for review against the leaflet, not a student handout.*

### A. The graphē

God’s design for a naturalistic Christian experience has always pillowed in the unleavened sincerity and truth of the graphē. The Lord answers from what is written (John 10:34-36; Psalm 82:6). The scripture cannot be broken. Holy Writ’s imprimatur governs persons, organizations, governments, and nations. Ersatz analogical, nonliteral religiousisms are a likeness that will not sit under the text; this school will not trade the writing for that likeness.

### B. The premiere convention

**The graphē is the premiere convention** for hearing from God and for spiritual growth. The leaflet/book/course is a straight issue on the cornerstone — first principles of the oracles of God (Hebrews 5:12) — under that convention. Growth is two movements: first principles, then maturity (perfection).

### C. Hebrews 5:11-14

Dull of hearing withholds meat. For the time they ought to be teachers; they need to be taught again; they have become such as need milk. Every one that uses milk is apeiros in the word of righteousness; he is a nēpios. Strong meat belongs to those of full age whose senses are exercised by use to discern good and evil.

### D. Diagnosis and cure

Absence of sound judgment + presence of unskill → aridity and tergiversation → damnable accepted heresies called doctrines. Blame: failure to know the Scriptures and align. Cure: spoudazō — intellectual labour; 2 Timothy 2:15 — approved workman, rightly dividing.

### E. Two marks and the aim

Babes: (1) dull of hearing; (2) unskilled in the Word, the basis for salvation. Knowledge = perception + learning + reasoning; practice = Word exercise / Word drill. Aim of the piece: productive living, control, godliness in all capacities — by skill in the word, not by slogan. Truth makes free where you continue in His word (John 8:31-36).
