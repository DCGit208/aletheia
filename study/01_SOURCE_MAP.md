## Page 1 — Title, oracles, babes, gate into repentance

### S1. Title and design of God

- **Title:** A concise definition for True Christ-ian Experience.
- God’s design for a *naturalistic* Christian experience (following the teachings, or manifesting the qualities or spirit, of Jesus Christ) has always rested in conformity with the unleavened sincerity and truth of the **graphē**: Holy Writ, Bible, Scripture.
- Contrast: “ersatz analogical (nonliteral) religiousisms.”
- **John 10:35** — Holy Writ: writings or sayings of unchallenged, unquestionable authority (imprimatur). This governs persons, organizations, governments, and nations.
- This leaflet is a straight issue on the **essential cornerstone**: foundation / first principles of the Oracles of God (**Hebrews 5:12**).
- It is the **premiere convention** for hearing from God and for spiritual growth.
- Growth is two movements, not one slogan: **1. First Principles → 2. Maturity (Perfection).**

### S2. Hebrews 5:12 quoted

Full KJV quotation: those who ought to be teachers need to be taught again the first principles of the oracles of God; they have become such as have need of **milk**, and not of **strong meat**.

### S3. Why organizations dry up

- Absence of **reasonability** (sound judgment) and presence of **unskillfulness** produce aridity and tergiversation among persons and even age-old organizations that appear Christianized.
- Result: “damnable accepted heresies called doctrines.”
- Cause: failure to realize the scholarly impetus of Scripture and to align accordingly; **failure to know the Scriptures** (soft or hard copy).
- Required labour: **spoudazō** — speed, effort, promptness, earnestness; diligence, endeavour, labour, **study, intellectually**. **2 Timothy 2:15**.
- Term: **apeiros** (Hebrews 5:13) — inexperienced, ignorant, unskilful.

### S4. The nēpios

- Anyone (person or organization) unskilled in the **word of righteousness** is a **nēpios**: simple-minded, immature Christian, babe, child.
- Hebrews 5:13 quoted with both Greek terms.
- **Two characteristics of babes:**
  1. Dull of hearing (the leaflet’s heading includes Hebrews 5:11; 5:11 is the “dull of hearing” verse).
  2. Unskilled in the Word, **which is the basis for salvation**.
- Knowledge defined: a mental result of **perception and learning and reasoning**. Practice named: **Word exercise, Word drill**.
- Aim of the piece: productive living, being in control, godliness in all capacities.
- **John 8:32** — Truth makes free.

### S5. Banner: First Principles — Hebrews 5:11–6:3

The six principles begin. Hebrews **6:3** (“And this will we do, if God permit”) is in the banner and is not developed in the body. See owner questions.

### S6. Repentance from dead works (begins)

- Heading: **Gr. Metanoia from nekros ergon**.
- Hebrews 6:1 quoted: “not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works…”
- **Metanoia** is **one of the eight original words for repentance**. The other seven are **not listed**.
- Metanoia specifies **compunction (remorse) for guilt including reformation**: regeneration, quickening, recreation — “New Creature” (**2 Corinthians 5:17**).
- Real change of mind and attitude over **sin and the cause**, not merely the **consequence**.
- Consequence named: **Hell**. Printed cluster: **Mt.16:19-31, Rev. 20:1015**. “Also by implication: reversal (of [another’s] decision).”
- **Nekros ergon (negra erga)** = works of the old man: spirit and nature of the devil (**Ephesians 4:22**); old things (**2 Corinthians 5:17**); old nature (**Ephesians 2:1** — trespasses = deviation from moral equity, and sins). Wages: death (**Romans 6:23**).
- Two kinds of “Old things” that **decease / pass away** (2 Corinthians 5:17):
  1. The spirit, nature, and power of sin — the old man — **nothing more nor less than the spirit and nature of the devil** (sentence continues on page 2).

## Page 3 — Three essential baptisms, Spirit witness, nine hands, gate into resurrection

### S13. Word as agency of new birth

- The washing signifies the **agency of the Word at new birth**. Also **John 3:5**.
- This is the **premiere** of the **three essential types of baptisms, out of seven mentioned in the scriptures for believers**.
- **Only three are named.** The other four are not listed.

### S14. Baptism 1 — into Christ and His body

- At repentance and new birth; fellowship of saints.
- “**One baptism**” (**Ephesians 4:5**) — the **only baptism that saves the soul**.
- **Galatians 3:27** quoted; **baptizō** = to make whelmed (fully wet); overpowered / overwhelmed; submerged / drowned. Baptized into Christ = **put on Christ**.
- Supporting: **Romans 6:3-7; Colossians 2:12; 1 Corinthians 12:13**.

### S15. Baptism 2 — water after one is saved

- Water **baptizō after one is saved**.
- Symbolizes the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ **already elucidated in baptism 1**.
- **1 Peter 3:21**.
- Source question: **Is it the water that saves us, or the thing that it is a figure or symbol of?**
- Faith in the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ saves the soul, **not the sign**. A sign points to a location; it is not the object.
- Water baptism does **not** save the soul from sin (**1 Corinthians 1:17-24**). Significant only for those who have received Christ.
- The **preaching of the cross**, not water baptism, is God’s power to save (**1 Corinthians 1:17-18, 24**).
- Definitive line: faith in Christ Jesus saves the soul, not water baptism. **Galatians 3:26** quoted.

### S16. Baptism 3 — in the Holy Spirit

- Natural / hereditary **witness of sonship** (heir of God).
- By implication from **baptizō**, **without measure** — **John 7:38** — “the measure Christ experienced” / **John 3:34**.
- **Fullness of God:** **Colossians 1:19; 2:9; 1 Corinthians 1:24; 12:27; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 2:16**. Full measure: **Ephesians 4:13**. Line: **The Spirit not by measure unto you.**
- **Galatians 4:6-7** quoted: because ye are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying Abba, Father.
- **Prerequisite:** becoming God’s son — new birth / salvation.
- The Holy Spirit **witnesses (informant)** a Father–Son relationship (**ensuant**) as a consequence of sonship / heir, **not slaves**.
- Not timidity: **not possible for you to fear**; you rather **are the fear and dread of the world** (thieves, wickedness, demons, Satan). Cluster: **Genesis 9:2 / Deuteronomy 11:25 / Jas.2:9**. Further reading: **Why We’re Beyond**.
- Instead: power, love, and a sound mind (**2 Timothy 1:7**).
- **John 16:13-14** quoted. **Anangellō** (“shall shew”) = announce in detail; declare, rehearse, report, show, speak, tell.
- “Mine” = the express image of God, announced unto you: express image and likeness of Me (**Genesis 1:26-28**).
- Further reading: **PRESENCE OF SONS — SPIRIT WITNESS** (its own book; not a volume of the later *Presence of Sons* 1–4 series).

## Page 4 — Material resurrection, chronology, five companies, gate into judgment

### S19. Spiritual resurrection is this-life only

- It takes place **only in this life** (**1 Corinthians 15:19**).
- When you leave this life / world, all possibilities of new-birth salvation or spiritual resurrection **have passed and are forfeited** if you are not yet born again.
- Cluster: **Ecclesiastes 11:1; Hebrews 9:27; Revelation 22:11**.

### S20. Material resurrection — the body, two issues

- Resurrection of the **body, not spirit**.
- **John 5:28-29** quoted in full: all in the graves hear His voice; good → resurrection of **life**; evil → resurrection of **damnation**.
- Two bodily resurrections, **1000 years between them** (**Revelation 20:4-6**):
  1. The **righteous to life before the Millennium**.
  2. The **wicked (unsaved / unregenerate) to damnation** (lake of fire: **Revelation 20:10-15**) **after the Millennium**.
- Printed support: **Daniel 12:3; John 5:28-29; Rev. 10:4-6, 11-15**, then immediately **Revelation 20:4-6**.

### S21. Chronology as the source states it (do not flatten)

Printed in a blue clarifying parenthesis:

- The **Millennium** = the one thousand years **after the tribulation**.
- The **tribulation** = **7 years after rapture 2**.
- **Rapture 2 is not Second Advent.**
- **Second advent** = coming of Christ **with the saints**, **after tribulation**, **ushering the Millennium**.
- Range given for that stretch: **Revelation 6–19**.

### S22. Facts and examples of resurrection

- Facts cluster: **Job 19:25-27; Isaiah 26:14-19; Psalm 16:10; John 20–21; 1 Peter 1:3; 3:21; Romans 1:4; 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15**.
- **Temporary** example: **Matthew 9:25**.
- **Permanent**: raised to immortality to live forever in their bodies. **Christ the firstfruits** of all permanent resurrections (**1 Corinthians 15:1-23**), and the **many saints now in heaven with their bodies** after His resurrection (**Matthew 27:52-53**).
- All who have part in the **first resurrection** rule with Christ **from the Millennium** (printed `Rev. 20:46` = 20:4-6).
- All **without Christ** are raised **together after the Millennium**, judged, and cast into the lake of fire forever (**Revelation 20:4-6, 11-15**).

The first **material** resurrection covers **various companies of the redeemed**. There are **5 raptures in the first resurrection**:

### S24. Eternal judgment (begins)

- Hebrews 6:2.
- **Matthew 25:46** — translated **eternal punishment**. Also **Revelation 14:9-11; 20:11-15; Isaiah 66:22-24**.
- **Revelation 20:15**: whosoever was not found written in the **book of life** was cast into the lake of fire. God is **no respecter of persons** (**Acts 10:34**). This is the **second death** (**Revelation 20:14**).
- God **blots the names of sinners out of His book** (**Exodus 32:32-33**). The Psalmist predicted it of **Judas** (**Psalm 69:20-28**). Christ warned of it for all who refuse to be born again (**John 3:36; Revelation 3:5; 22:18-19**, printed `22:1819`).
- “Dead” in the Bible predominantly, if not definitely, means man’s **separation or cutting off from realizing God’s purpose** (**Genesis 1:26-28**) for which he was created.

### S25. Three kinds of death

1. **Physical death** — separation of inner man from outer man: soul and spirit from the body. **James 2:26**.
2. **Spiritual death** — separation of man from God because of sin. **Matthew 8:22; Colossians 2:14; 1 Timothy 5:6**.
3. **Second death** — second and **eternal** separation from God in the lake of fire. **Revelation 20:14; 2:11; 20:6, 14; 21:8**.

- The **soul and spirit of man is immortal**.
- **All** Scriptures on resurrection of the dead, without exception, refer to **bodies** which die and will be resurrected from dust again. Cluster: **Daniel 12:2; John 5:28-29; 1 Corinthians 15; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-17; Revelation 20:4-6, 11-15**.

### S26. Five prisons — not the grave (begins)

There are **five distinct prisons in the underworld of the departed**; **not one is the same as the grave**.

1. **Tartarus** — **1 Peter 3:19; 2 Peter 2:4** (Greek named); **Jude 6-7**. Special prison for **fallen angels who sinned before the flood**. **No human beings or demons here.**
2. **Paradise** — **Luke 16:19-31; 23:43**. Abode of the righteous at physical death, held captive by Satan against their will, until Christ conquered death, hell, and the grave. **Now empty of the righteous**, who go to heaven at death, since Christ captured the captives and took them to heaven when He ascended (**Luke 16:22; Ephesians 4:8; Hebrews 2:15**).
3. **Hell** — torment compartment of **sheol-hades** where wicked souls go (continues on page 5).
