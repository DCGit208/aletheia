### B2. Seven baptisms; three for believers; three agents

**Seven in Scripture**

1. John’s baptism in water  
2. Christ’s baptism in water  
3. Baptism in suffering (Luke 12:50)  
4. Baptism in the cloud and in the sea (1 Corinthians 10:2)  
5. Christian baptism in water  
6. Baptism into Christ and into His body  
7. Baptism in the Holy Spirit  

**Three for believers** = 6, 5, and 7 in that doctrinal order (into Christ at new birth; water after one is saved; Spirit).

- Into Christ = “one baptism” (Ephesians 4:5): the only baptism that saves the soul and brings into the body.  
- Water after one is saved.  
- Spirit baptism = enduement of power for service; can be **before** water (Acts 10:44-48) or **after** it (Acts 1:4-8; 2:1-11; 8:12-21; 19:1-7). The leaflet’s **witness of sonship** stays; this enduement is taught with it, not instead of it.

**Agents**

- The Holy Spirit baptizes into Christ and into His body (1 Corinthians 12:13).  
- Christ baptizes in the Holy Spirit (Matthew 3:11; John 1:31-33).  
- The minister baptizes into water (Matthew 28:19).  

*Baptizō* (G907) from *baptō* (G911): to dip; to overwhelm with suffering; to bury into — regardless of the element.

### C3 — what 1 Corinthians 15:19 means

> “If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are of all men most miserable.”

Paul’s argument in 15:12-19: if there is **no resurrection of the dead**, Christ is not raised; preaching and faith are vain; the dead in Christ are perished. Therefore, if our hope in Christ were limited to **this present life only** (no bodily resurrection, no future), we would be the most miserable of men — we would have suffered for a dead Christ.

He is **not** using the verse to define new birth. He is proving **material / bodily** resurrection.

The leaflet printed 15:19 next to “spiritual resurrection takes place only in this life.” That is a different use. You have now said the this-life window is **Hebrews 9:27** (it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment) and **Revelation 22:11** (he that is unjust, let him be unjust still…). Those two carry “no new birth after death.”

**What I will teach:** 15:19 with the facts-file and the hope of the body (Course 5). The forfeiture of new birth after death from Hebrews 9:27 and Revelation 22:11 (A2). I will not make 15:19 mean the opposite of Paul’s argument.
