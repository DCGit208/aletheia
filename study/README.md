# Foundation School — pre-curriculum study

This folder is the **end-to-end study** of Aletheia Libraries 1 (2015), the source manuscript for Foundation School: *The Principles of the Doctrine of Christ*.

It is **not** the textbook and **not** the 80–100 written lessons. Those follow owner review of this study.

## Source

- Writer archive of the printed leaflet (not a student resource, not in Shop): `study/source/aletheia-libraries-1.pdf` and `study/source/leaflet-extracted.txt`. Do not publish the leaflet in the app.
- Working extract of the PDF text layer: `study/source/leaflet-extracted.txt`
- Author of the teaching: Dr. Coach Achu Gustave
- Imprint of the later textbook: Aletheia Publications

Holy Scripture is the biblical authority. The leaflet is the school’s source manuscript: Scripture quotations, references, word studies, doctrinal teaching, and the author’s organized presentation of those Scriptures.

## Method used

1. Read all five pages of the leaflet (text layer + rendered pages).
2. Extract every heading, claim, term, distinction, question, further-reading title, and printed citation.
3. Study each cited passage in the KJV (the leaflet’s English), in its immediate context, and as part of the **cluster** the leaflet builds.
4. Preserve the source’s sequences. Do not replace them with another tradition.
5. Where a printed citation is compressed (`Rev. 20:1015`) or does not verse-match the claim when read in isolation, **record the print**, study the cluster, and send the ambiguity to `10_OWNER_QUESTIONS.md`. Do not silently “fix” the theology.

Greek and Hebrew are studied as the leaflet uses them, plus the owner-supplied lists in `11_OWNER_DECISIONS.md` (seven words for repent; seven baptisms and three agents). Printed citation errors the owner corrected (Luke 16:19-31, not Matthew 16; Revelation 7:1-8; Genesis 9:2 and Deuteronomy 11:25 for dread) are taught as corrected. See `11_OWNER_DECISIONS.md`.

## What this study will not do

- Write the hardcover or the courseware
- Add a seventh Year-1 course
- Merge *Spirit Witness* into *Presence of Sons*
- Merge *Why We’re Beyond* into *Fearfully Made*
- Collapse several raptures into one event
- Collapse second advent into rapture 2
- Treat The Walk and The Intensive as two doctrines
