## 6. Expansion method (what “exhaust to the core” means)

The leaflet is the **compressed source of truth**. Course writers do not paraphrase it into two stub lessons. For every knot in the manuscript they produce a teachable unit:

**Quote → word (Greek/Hebrew as the leaflet uses it) → scripture cluster → distinction → written charge.**

Rules:

- Do not add a seminary word-list the leaflet does not use.
- Do not skip a distinction the leaflet makes (three baptisms; nine occasions of hands; two resurrections; five companies; three deaths; five prisons).
- Do not treat a further-reading title as a seventh course.
- Do not import another network’s course template, pricing, or roles.
- Questions the leaflet itself asks (What is an heir? How would you liken John 17:20–23?) become student charges, not slogans.

**Planned scale:** about **12–18 lessons per course**, **~80–100 lessons** for the year. The Walk uses the same lesson list at ~3 hours/week; Intensive assigns more pages, more drill, and a longer charge on the same lessons.

## 7. The six courses

### Course 1 — Repentance from Dead Works

**Hebrews 6:1 · 9 weeks · $100**

Compressed source: *metanoia* from *nekros ergon*; seven original words for repent (owner list in `study/11_OWNER_DECISIONS.md`); reformation, not consequence-only; two “old things” (old man / outward sin); Christ the only way; Receive Him (John 1:12; Romans 10:8–9).

**Exhaust:** Hebrews 5:11–14 (ought to be teachers; milk/meat); *apeiros* and *nēpios*; two marks of babes; *spoudazō* and 2 Timothy 2:15; word drill; cause vs Hell-as-consequence; each scripture list for old man and for outward sin; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Acts 4:12; Hebrews 9:14; the further-reading volume *The Power of Salvation*.

**Companion book:** *The Power of Salvation*

---

### Course 2 — Faith Toward God

**Hebrews 6:1 · 9 weeks · $100**

Compressed source: *pistis* as credence, sonship, adoption; *Elohim*; Genesis 1:26–27; image, likeness, glory come short; free justification; *dikaioō* / *dikaiosynē*; *apolytrōsis*; heir; John 17.

**Exhaust:** every question the leaflet asks (Has man been justified? Who is heir? What does heir mean? How liken John 17:20–23?); 2 Corinthians 4:4 and 4:6; 2 Peter 1:4; Psalm 82:6; 1 Corinthians 11:7.

**Companion books:** *The Doctrine of Man* (already in the house); *Why Men Die*; *True Nature of God*; *Who’s Jesus to You?*

---

### Course 3 — The Doctrine of Baptisms

**Hebrews 6:2 · 8 weeks · $100**

Compressed source: *baptismos* / *baptizō*; washing by the Word; three essential baptisms among seven in Scripture; only baptism into Christ saves the soul; water as sign, not power; Spirit as witness of sonship, without measure.

**Exhaust:** Ephesians 5:26; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:5; Ephesians 4:5; Galatians 3:26–27; Romans 6:3–7; 1 Corinthians 1:17–24; John 7:38; John 3:34; Colossians 1:19; 2:9; Galatians 4:6–7; John 16:13–14; *anaggellō*; Genesis 1:26–28 as the leaflet uses it of “Mine.”

**Companion books (each its own Book record):**

| Title | Must not be confused with |
| --- | --- |
| *Spirit Witness* | **Not** a volume of the *Presence of Sons* series. If the leaflet listed it near that series, that proximity is bibliographic, not a series assignment. |
| *Why We’re Beyond* | **Not** *Fearfully Made*. Two different books. |

---

### Course 4 — Laying on of Hands

**Hebrews 6:2 · 8 weeks · $100**

Compressed source: nine biblical occasions (blessing men, offerings, ordaining, imparting Spirit, blessing children, healing, miracles, imparting gifts, imparting Spirit baptism). Resultant, not a substitute for the foundation.

**Exhaust:** each of the nine texts as its own study; why the leaflet says this is viable only after the more grounding principles; no invented extra volume (the leaflet names none here).

**Companion books:** none required by the leaflet body.

---

### Course 5 — Resurrection of the Dead

**Hebrews 6:2 · 9 weeks · $100**

Compressed source: spiritual vs material resurrection; new birth only in this life; two material resurrections (life / damnation); Millennium as the leaflet defines it; temporary vs permanent; firstfruits; five companies / raptures in the first resurrection.

**Exhaust:** Ephesians 2:1–6; Hebrews 9:27 and Revelation 22:11 (new birth only in this life); 1 Corinthians 15:19 with bodily hope, not as that proof-text; John 5:28–29; Daniel 12; Revelation 20; Job 19:25–27; Isaiah 26:14–19; Matthew 9:25 vs 1 Corinthians 15:1–23; Matthew 27:52–53; each of the five companies with its texts; tribulation / rapture 2 / second advent **as the leaflet orders them**.

**Chronology (studied, not reopened):** rapture 2 is not the second advent; tribulation is seven years after rapture 2; second advent is Christ with the saints after tribulation, ushering the Millennium. Five companies complete the first resurrection. See `study/05_CHRONOLOGY_MAP.md`.

**Companion book:** *Millennium*

---

### Course 6 — Eternal Judgment

**Hebrews 6:2 · 9 weeks · $100**

Compressed source: eternal punishment; book of life; blotting of names; three kinds of death; soul/spirit immortal, body raised; five prisons of the underworld, none identical with the grave.

**Exhaust:** Matthew 25:46; Revelation 14 and 20; Exodus 32:32–33; Psalm 69:20–28; John 3:36; Revelation 3:5; Genesis 1:26–28 as purpose cut off; James 2:26; Tartarus, Paradise (now empty of the righteous), Hell/Sheol-Hades, the Abyss/Abaddon, Lake of Fire/Gehenna — each with the leaflet’s texts and the leaflet’s “who is here / who is not.”

**Companion books:** none required by the leaflet body. Back-list titles may be linked later, not as Year 1 courses.
