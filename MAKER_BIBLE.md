## Central proposition (exact)

The central proposition of MAKER BIBLE — Lordship Edition is not simply “God created man.” It is the purpose embedded in the way God created man.

The governing teleology

The Maker made man in His image and likeness so that man would exercise dominion over the works of His hands.

Genesis 1:26–28 gives the sequence explicitly:

Image → likeness → blessing → fruitfulness → subduing → dominion.

And Psalm 8 confirms the same design:

“Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet.”
